# Listening notes: ievan-polkka-s1

Run `ievan_polkka-s1-220a0105`: Ievan Polkka, seed 1, default configuration. Best fitness 0.929
after 550 generations.

- What to listen for: the change of texture between the parts. The first A part holds the chord
  root on P2 over a walking triangle bass and a half-time beat. The B parts switch to fast
  arpeggios (32nd notes the first time, 16th notes the second time) with a sixteenth-note beat.
  A snare fill closes every 4-bar phrase.
- What works: the A/B contrast makes the form audible, and the walking bass keeps the polka
  moving. The student liked the result on first listening.
- What does not work: P2 and TR never rest, and the General MIDI square lead only approximates
  the NES pulse timbre.
