DCC831 Generative AI for Music · UFMG 2026/2 · Assignment 1

A genetic algorithm that arranges songs for the NES sound chip

Every song has three renderings: the original input, the generated arrangement with General MIDI timbres, and the same arrangement on our NES chip synthesizer. The video plays the chip version and draws each channel as a glowing line.

For an ABC tune, the original is the lead sheet played plainly. For a MIDI song, it is the fan-made MIDI file itself, rendered with the same soundfont and cut to the same bars.
