A genetic algorithm that arranges songs for the NES sound chip

DCC831 Generative AI for Music (UFMG, 2026/2), Assignment 1. Every song has three renderings: the original input, the generated arrangement with General MIDI timbres, and the same arrangement on our NES chip synthesizer. The video plays the chip version and draws each channel as a glowing line.

For an ABC tune, the original is the lead sheet played plainly. For a MIDI song, the original is the fan-made MIDI file itself, rendered with the same soundfont and cut to the same bars. Code, method and paper: GiowGiow/chiptune-ga-arranger.

P1 pulse: melody P2 pulse: harmony TR triangle: bass NO noise: drums
The audio is rendered only from MIDI files: FluidSynth with the FluidR3_GM soundfont, or the project's own chip synthesizer (pulse, triangle and noise waveforms).
