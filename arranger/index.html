NES Arranger

Give it a lead sheet or a MIDI song. A genetic algorithm evolves an arrangement for the four channels of the NES sound chip, and you hear it next to the original. Your file stays on this device.

P1 pulse · melody P2 pulse · harmony TR triangle · bass NO noise · drums
1Input 2Settings 3Evolution 4Result Catalog About

1Input

Drop an ABC or MIDI file here, or pick one of the examples.

2Settings

The seed, the size of the run and the fitness weights. The defaults are the command line's.

3Evolution

The fitness curve and the plan of every bar appear here while the run evolves.

4Result

Play the arrangement on the NES chip, compare it with the original, and download the MIDI, the settings and the lead sheet.

MPB MIDI catalog

Search a catalog of Brazilian popular music by title, composer or performer, download a song from its site and drop it in above.

About

How the arranger works: a plan of four genes per bar, scored by seven criteria, evolved by the same genetic algorithm as the project's command line.
