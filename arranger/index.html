About

The arranger is the browser version of the project's command line (chiptune-ga-arranger, DCC831 TP1, UFMG). It reads your song into a lead sheet: the melody, the chord of every moment and the parts (verse, chorus). A genetic algorithm then searches for an arrangement plan: four genes per bar, the texture of the second pulse channel (P2), the bass texture of the triangle (TR), the drum pattern of the noise channel (NO) and the octave of the melody (P1). Seven music-theory criteria score each plan, roulette selection with elitism picks parents, one-point crossover at bar lines and per-gene mutation make children, and the run stops when the best plan stops improving.

Same seed, same arrangement. The app has its own seeded random generator, so the same song, seed and settings give the same arrangement in any browser. The command line uses numpy's generator: the same seed there gives a different arrangement, equally valid. The algorithm, the settings and the fitness are the same, and tests check that both score a plan identically.

Your files stay here. Everything runs in this page: nothing you drop is uploaded, and the app fetches only its own files.
